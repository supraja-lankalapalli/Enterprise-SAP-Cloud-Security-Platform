# Business Problem

SL Cloud Technologies is a growing global organization that supports multiple business departments, including Finance, Human Resources, Procurement, Sales, Information Technology, Security Operations, Internal Audit, and Executive Management. As the company continues to expand, the number of employees requiring access to SAP applications has increased significantly.

Currently, user accounts, business roles, and access requests are managed through manual administrative processes. Although these processes have supported the organization, they are becoming increasingly difficult to manage as the business grows.

The security team has identified several challenges that require improvement:

- Users may receive more access than required for their job responsibilities.
- Manual role assignments increase the risk of inconsistent access across departments.
- User provisioning and access changes can take longer than expected, affecting business productivity.
- Access requests are difficult to track from submission through approval.
- Security teams have limited visibility into user activities and access changes.
- Audit preparation requires significant manual effort due to scattered security records.
- Maintaining compliance with internal security policies becomes more challenging as the organization expands.

To address these challenges, SL Cloud Technologies requires a centralized SAP Security and Identity Governance solution that improves user administration, standardizes role management, strengthens access governance, and provides greater visibility into security activities across the enterprise.
